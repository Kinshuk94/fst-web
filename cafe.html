Evenings

After the sun goes down

The days are for exploring. The evenings are for the tribe.

Most nights there's something happening — a film on the projector, a guitarist who wandered in, or just a bonfire in the orchard with chai and conversation. These evenings are never planned. They just happen, the way the best evenings do.
