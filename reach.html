Getting here

How to reach us

Naggar is on the left bank of the Beas, 22km south of Manali. We're on Pulag Road, 400m past the Roerich Art Gallery.

1
Fly to Kullu-Manali Airport (KUU) — 38km from Naggar. Flights available from Delhi (~1 hr), Chandigarh, and other hubs. Book via IndiGo, Air India, or SpiceJet.
2
Taxi from airport to Naggar — approximately ₹1,200–₹1,500 for the ~1.5 hr journey. Hire a taxi from the airport stand and ask for Naggar / Roerich Art Gallery.
3
Alternatively: shared taxi to Kullu + local bus to Naggar (₹200–300 total, ~2 hrs). Buses run frequently from Kullu bus stand to Naggar.
4
From Naggar bus stop — continue 400m up Pulag Road past the Roerich Art Gallery. Look for our wooden sign on the right.
💡 Tip: Flights to KUU are weather-dependent and can be cancelled. If you're on a tight schedule, build in a buffer day or consider the bus.
1
Delhi → Manali overnight bus — HRTC Volvo buses depart from Delhi ISBT Kashmere Gate at ~5–6 PM. Journey: ~14 hrs. Arrive Manali bus stand by morning. Book at hrtchp.com or the counter.
2
Chandigarh → Manali — 8–9 hrs, multiple HRTC departures daily. Good option if coming from Delhi by train first.
3
Manali bus stand → Naggar — Local buses run every 30–60 min (₹40–60, ~45 min). Or take a shared taxi (₹80–120 per seat). Tell the driver you're going to Naggar / Roerich Art Gallery.
4
From Naggar bus stop — walk 400m up Pulag Road, past the Roerich Art Gallery on your left. Our wooden gate sign is on the right.
💡 Tip: The overnight bus from Delhi is the most popular route for backpackers. Book a window seat on the right side for the best mountain views on arrival.
1
Delhi → Naggar (~530km) — Take NH44 to Chandigarh, then NH3 (Chandigarh–Manali Highway) through Bilaspur, Mandi, and Kullu. Total ~12–14 hrs depending on conditions. Start early to avoid mountain road night driving.
2
At Patlikuhl (30km before Manali) — turn left at the T-junction onto the left-bank road. Follow signs for Naggar. This road is quieter and more scenic than the main highway.
3
Into Naggar — follow the road through Naggar village. Pass the road junction for Naggar Castle. Continue until you see the Roerich Art Gallery on your left.
4
400m past the Gallery — turn into Pulag Road. Our wooden gate sign is on the right. Small parking area available.
💡 Tip: GPS can be unreliable in the hills. Use "Roerich Art Gallery Naggar" as your destination and ask locals once you're in the village.
